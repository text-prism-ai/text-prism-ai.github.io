Limitations & how to interpret this (read before trusting a result)

AI-text detection is probabilistic classification, not forensic proof of authorship. This tool cannot reliably say "this exact sentence was written by ChatGPT" — it reports how closely the text's statistical patterns resemble the model's AI-written training examples.

False positives are more likely on: formulaic writing, academic/technical writing, non-native English, heavily edited text, and very short passages.

False negatives are more likely after: paraphrasing, translation, heavy human editing, or rewriting by another model.

The "fast" detector (TMR / RoBERTa-base, trained on the RAID benchmark) reports strong published accuracy, but was evaluated on specific domains (news, Wikipedia, reviews, recipes, Reddit, etc.) — performance on unrelated domains isn't guaranteed.

The "thorough" detector (based on desklib/ai-text-detector-v1.01) might be expected to work better with academic texts.

The AI-likelihood bands shown are heuristic labels, not thresholds validated against your specific text distribution.
